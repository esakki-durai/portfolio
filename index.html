About Us

We help businesses go digital with reliable websites and tailored software.

Company introduction

Zigi Softwares is a software and web development company focused on dependable, scalable solutions for growing businesses. We pair clear communication with disciplined engineering so your product launches on schedule and remains straightforward to own and extend.

What we do

We design and build business websites, web applications, e-commerce stores, ERP and CRM systems, and industry-specific software for hospitals, schools, and billing. We also integrate third-party APIs and provide website maintenance and support.

Mission

Deliver dependable digital products that simplify operations and grow with our clients’ businesses.

Vision

To be a long-term technology partner known for quality, transparency, and support.
